About

ZigFlames is a producer and composer from New York creating cinematic hip-hop, piano-driven instrumentals, and emotionally charged urban soundtracks.

From Elmont, NY. One-man-band: years of ideas finished in the basement — the story behind Basement Cipher — then sent into a catalog of original hip-hop and R&B for artists, sync licensing, film, television, and media.

The sound sits between streetlight and score: modern trap textures, piano, and late-night atmosphere. Debut album ZigFlames Vol. 1 is on major streaming platforms. Vol. 2, Desperado Music Beats, continues that line — noir, rain, and motion.
