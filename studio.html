Your imagination. Your machine.

A customizable prompt-building machine where you can create your own categories, add your own words, and build reusable palettes around any world you imagine.

Why it exists

I created the Prompt Machine because I had ideas, but I didn’t always have the exact words to communicate them to ChatGPT, Grok, or an image bot. I started organizing words into clickable palettes. Those palettes kept growing until they became a complete creative system.

Create a car world. Build a hip-hop universe. Develop characters, environments, camera styles, moods, fashion, or categories entirely your own.
